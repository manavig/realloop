The wall every team hits

The usual ways of getting human judgment onto a production AI all fail in the same place.

Internal bandwidth

Works at low volume. Stops scaling the moment the product does.

Hiring a workforce

Slow and hard. And once hired, making ordinary people fast and accurate at judgment work is its own unsolved problem.

LLM judges alone

Scale infinitely, but their output cannot be trusted on its own · and some things they cannot judge at all.

Quality at scale is a workflow problem, not a hiring problem

Agencies scale headcount but cannot hold quality. LLM judges scale but cannot be trusted. RealLoop breaks a fuzzy judgment into objective sub-tasks an ordinary reviewer can execute accurately, screens and trains reviewers against those exact sub-tasks, and calibrates machine judges for the rest. That is what makes quality affordable · and affordable quality is what makes the market servable.

One fuzzy question, decomposed

"Are my agent's order confirmation calls actually going well?"

Human Does the transcript match what the customer actually said?

Why the human checks are load-bearing

Transcript: "हां जी" · yes

Customer said: "नहीं नहीं अभी तोह नहीं चाहिए" · no, not right now.

A yes that was a no, on a call whose only job was to confirm the order. The transcript read as agreement. A reviewer heard the refusal.

After you say start

What happens with your request

The reviewers already exist · the marketplace keeps them screened and ready. Your use case goes through two stages, each one measurable.

Stage 1

Screening and training

We break your problem into objective sub-tasks and match reviewers whose languages and skills fit your calls. They are screened again on those exact sub-tasks, using your real production data, and trained with instant feedback until they hit the bar.

Stage 2

Judgment logging

The panel goes live on your calls, logging judgments in an interface generated for your checks · with the panel's reliability measured and published to you on every batch.

What you get back

Not a spreadsheet of flags. The agent ranked by how much it needs attention, the one root cause to fix first, and every finding as a line you can play.

portal.realloop.in/portal/agents

Every row is the transcript against what the customer actually said, with the audio behind it. This is the one thing a machine judge scoring a transcript can never catch · it is scoring the same audio it already misheard.

And how much to trust it, published

Expert-rated calls stay seeded in every batch, unmarked. We measure how often reviewers agree with each other and how often they match that hidden expert, per agent, and show you the number next to the findings it produced. Most vendors cannot tell you their reliability. It is a tab in ours.
